Analysis
========================================

**Throughput Comparison:**
- YCSB: ~25,244 ops/sec (load phase)
- uuid-benchmark: ~97,560 rec/sec (actual, not displayed)

**uuid-benchmark 3.9x FASTER** - Why?
1. **Batch size:** uuid-benchmark uses batch=100 (100 records per transaction)
   - YCSB uses individual transactions
   - Batching dramatically reduces commit overhead
2. **pgbench optimization:** Prepared statements, connection pooling
3. **Different client implementation**

**Normalized comparison (single-row inserts):**
- YCSB: ~25,244 ops/sec (single inserts)
- uuid-benchmark: ~97,560/100 ≈ 975 transactions/sec (if unbatched)
- Expected: Both should be ~20-30k ops/sec for single inserts
